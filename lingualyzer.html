--- layout: single title: Lingualyzer permalink: /lingualyzer/ author_profile: true classes: wide --- During my PhD, we built Lingualyzer, an easily accessible tool specifically designed for interdisciplinary researchers without advanced computational linguistic knowledge or programming skills. Lingualyzer analyzes text at three different text levels (sentence, paragraph, document), and includes 351 multidimensional linguistic measures available in 41 different languages, allowing for a wide variety of applications within fields, ranging from psycholinguistics, social psychology, cognitive psychology, education, to literary studies. You can read more about the tool here. The Supplementary Material can be downloaded here.
